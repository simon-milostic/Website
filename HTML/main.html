INTEREST BURDEN

An official United Nations report examining the issue of debt in developing countries highlights a positive-feedback loop between financing future investments and servicing existing debt. The analysis builds up to an alarming fact: approximately 3.3 billion people live in countries that spend more on interest payments than on education or healthcare. Read more at Arise News.

TOTAL DEBT

A reader-friendly document presented by the United Nations covering debt burden of developing countries. The article points out the difference between developed and developing countries, using simple and accessible graphs. Moreover, it covers the issue of reliance on private creditors and the associated risks. Finally, the article highlights the global inequality using numerical data. This article is highly recommended. Read more at the United Nations.
